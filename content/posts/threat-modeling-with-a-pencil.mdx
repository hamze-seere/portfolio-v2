The best threat models I have run started as drawings on paper, not rows in a spreadsheet.

Drawing a data flow forces the room to agree on what the system *is* before arguing about what could go wrong. Boundaries become lines; trust becomes arrows crossing them.

## The whole method

- Draw the boxes: users, services, data stores.
- Draw the lines between them.
- For every line that crosses a trust boundary, ask: *what crosses here, and who could fake it?*

The threats arrive on their own. The spreadsheet comes later, if at all.
